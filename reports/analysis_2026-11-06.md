## 2. Data and method

| Item | Value |
|---|---|
| Database | `data/betbrain.db` |
| Finished matches | 12,277 |
| Scheduled matches | 12 (all dated 2026-11-03/04; feed stale) |
| Odds rows | 61,438 (Bet365 1X2 and Over/Under 2.5) |
| Development seasons | 2021/22 – 2025/26 |
| Development cutoff | 2026-07-01 |
| Walk-forward out-of-sample window | 2022-08-01 → 2026-07-01, 30-day blocks |
| Walk-forward matches | 9,279 |
| Priced picks (Home / Away / Over 2.5) | 27,837 |
| Holdout | 2026/27, 609 finished matches |

**Walk-forward design.** 2021/22 is used only as the initial training season. From 2022-08-01 the
model is retrained before each 30-day block on everything earlier, then predicts the block. This gives
four complete out-of-sample seasons (2022/23–2025/26) and never touches 2026/27.

**Priced markets.** The edge test uses only markets with a real Bet365 price and a direct model
probability: `HOME_WIN`, `AWAY_WIN`, `OVER_2_5`. This is the same set the production `edge-test`
uses. Derived double chances and estimated Over 1.5 / BTTS prices are excluded. `UNDER_2_5` is not a
model pick (the production engine never selects it); its odds are used only for the Over/Under
overround in A3.

**Gate and Brier.** The primary gate is a 5% minimum edge (`model_prob × odds − 1 > 0.05`). ROI is
flat-stake per unit; the 95% CI is a 2,000-resample bootstrap, identical to `betbrain/edge.py`.
Brier is the multiclass 1X2 Brier score: mean over matches of `Σ_k (p_k − y_k)²`.

## 4. A2 — Ablation without `mkt_*` features

The model was retrained with the five market-implied features removed (`mkt_h`, `mkt_d`, `mkt_a`,
`mkt_o25`, `mkt_o15`); 24 features remain. Work-forward window, blocks, odds and gate are identical.

| Predictor | Brier (1X2) | Difference vs market |
|---|---:|---:|
| Market only | **0.5875** | — |
| Full model (reference) | 0.6029 | +0.0154 |
| No-`mkt_*` model | 0.6174 | +0.0299 (worse) |

Removing the market features makes calibration worse, not better. The model has **no independent
signal** that compensates for losing the market's view.

| Minimum edge | Bets | ROI per unit | 95% CI |
|---:|---:|---:|---|
| > 0% | 12,535 | −11.3% | [−13.9%, −8.5%] |
| > 3% | 11,073 | −11.9% | [−15.1%, −8.9%] |
| **> 5% (primary gate)** | **10,176** | **−12.7%** | **[−15.9%, −9.3%]** |
| > 10% | 8,180 | −13.6% | [−17.3%, −9.6%] |

The no-market model bets more often (10,176 vs 7,875 at the 5% gate) and does slightly worse. This is
the signature of overconfident, uninformative probabilities, not a hidden edge.

## 5. A3 — Bet365 vs best-of-market vs Pinnacle

`MaxH/MaxD/MaxA`, `PSH/PSD/PSA`, `Max>2.5` and `Max<2.5` were read from the football-data.co.uk CSVs
for seasons 2021/22–2025/26 (30 files, none missing). They were used **only to re-price the same
picks**; they were never model features.

### Average overround

| Market | Bet365 | Best-of-market | Pinnacle |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| 1X2 | 5.59% | 0.93% | 3.20% |
| Over/Under 2.5 | 4.95% | 1.81% | n/a |

“Best-of-market” takes the highest price for each outcome, so it is not a single bookmaker and its
overround is not directly comparable; it is the theoretical best available price.

### Same picks at the 5% gate (7,875 picks)

| Market | Picks | Bet365 ROI | Best-of-market ROI | Pinnacle ROI |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Home win | 2,810 | −12.7% | −8.3% | −10.0% (2,489) |
| Away win | 3,529 | −13.8% | −7.6% | −14.3% (3,075) |
| Over 2.5 | 1,536 | −7.4% | −4.6% | n/a |
| **All (1X2 + Over 2.5)** | **7,875** | **−12.2%** | **−7.3%** | **−12.4%** (1X2 only, 5,564) |

Shopping for the best price removes about **4.9 percentage points** of loss, roughly the Bet365
margin. It does **not** turn the strategy positive: the best-price portfolio still returns −7.3%.
The problem is pick quality, not only the margin.

## 8. Overall findings

1. **The market beats the model on accuracy.** Brier: market 0.5875 vs full model 0.6029 vs
   no-market model 0.6174. The model is a lossy copy of the market.
2. **No edge at any development gate.** At the primary 5% gate the full model returns −12.2%
   (95% CI [−15.9%, −8.4%]); the no-market model returns −12.7%.
3. **Best-price shopping is not enough.** Re-pricing the same picks at the best available odds
   improves ROI by about 4.9 points but still leaves −7.3%.
4. **The frozen holdout fails the pre-registered gate.** −2.2% over 438 bets, 95% CI
   [−17.0%, +12.9%]. The stop rule is triggered.
5. **Do not stake money, and stop betting-related development** under the pre-registered rule.
   Part B (API-Football) remains unverified because the key was not exposed to this environment.
